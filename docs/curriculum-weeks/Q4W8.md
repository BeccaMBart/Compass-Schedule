# Q4W8 — Curriculum Guide Extract

Q4W8 Weekly preparation

Complete  demonstrate  and hand over the product

Starting point: The finished candidate and operating guides are ready for final verification.

End-of-week check: All required acceptance checks pass; the permanent app and ownership handoff are complete.

Explain these ideas before the build

Completion means the agreed product works and someone owns its continued operation. The final demonstration is evidence, not a substitute for testing. Students should explain basic concepts, a prompt improvement, a project-management decision, and a verified contribution. Refinements may continue afterward, but required features, Azure teaching evidence, and permanent VPS operation are due this week.

acceptance: A recorded decision that agreed requirements are met.

maintenance: Ongoing care after release.

reflection: Explaining what was learned from evidence.

Prepare this week in order

1. Confirm the previous checkpoint works. Reserve Monday for final checks and urgent required repairs, Tuesday for demonstration, and Wednesday for handoff/assessment. Have the adult owner confirm ongoing hosting, backups, and support.

2. Prepare the fictional records or paper examples named above. Prepare account/tool access only; do not implement this week’s app changes before the live lesson.

3. Practice this project-management action with students: Close the project against the agreed scope. Transfer the nonblocking backlog and operations responsibility to the named adult owner.

4. Improve the prompt by focusing on this skill: Ask AI to summarize only verified outcomes and separate evidence from assumptions. Students check the summary for unsupported claims.

5. Keep the three lessons below in order. If a check fails, use that lesson’s repair prompt and preserve the working data. Record failed or not-run checks honestly.

Student record for the week

Each student keeps one note with their name/pseudonym, role, prediction, small change or review, observed result, and one concept in their own words. Use docs/student-work/ for class evidence. The teacher remains responsible for the application and all account credentials.
